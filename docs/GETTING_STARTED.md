# Create and run a light show

1. Open **Light Show Studio** and choose **New Light Show**. Click the title to name it. Use the **+** in Fixtures to add moving heads, switched plugs, and RGB strips. Each fixture gets a free starting address, with a simulated controller added as needed.
2. Select a fixture to change its controller, channels, or group. Drag fixtures around **Fixture Layout** to match the stage or house. Choose **File → Save Fixture Setup** to reuse the patch and positions in another show.
3. Open **Soundtrack…** to add a song. Select a fixture, audition its state, and choose **Looks → Capture**. Select a group or **All Fixtures** to capture several states together. **Place Cue** adds a look at the playhead. **Motion & Chases** opens movement keys and chase programming.
4. Click **Record Cues**, then trigger a saved look or press **GO** during playback. Click **Stop Recording**, review or adjust the captured times, and add selected cues to the timeline. Use the **Cues** menu to find manual-only cues.
5. Choose **File → Save Light Show**. The `.lightshow` package includes the song. Reopen it through Project Home. In **Show Mode**, choose **Check & Arm**, then use **Play**, **Stop**, **GO**, and **Blackout** with the simulated fixture monitor.

On the Home screen, **New Show from Setup** starts an independent show with the saved fixture configuration. Direct hardware output requires a future device-specific implementation and testing.
